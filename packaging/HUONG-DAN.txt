GtaMiniGameBot - ban portable
=============================

Cach chay
---------
1. Giai nen CA thu muc ra mot cho bat ky (Desktop, D:\tools, ...).
   Phai giu nguyen ca cac file va thu muc con, dung tach rieng file exe ra.
2. Chay GtaMiniGameBot.exe. Khong can cai .NET, tat ca da nam trong exe.
3. Windows co the bao "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen) vi exe chua ky.
   Bam "More info" -> "Run anyway".
4. Nen chay bang quyen Administrator neu game khong nhan phim/chuot.

Yeu cau
-------
- Windows 10/11 64-bit.
- Game chay o che do cua so hoac borderless.
- Vung da khoanh san (job cau ca) canh cho man hinh 2560x1440. Man hinh khac do
  phan giai thi van chay duoc nhung phai vao app khoanh lai vung cho dung.

Da khoanh san nhung gi
----------------------
Zip nay kem san toan bo vung + anh mau cua job CAU CA cho 2560x1440: thanh keo ca,
mau con ca, nut Keep, cac o tui do / cop xe, menu radial va bo mau chu so de doc
can nang. Chay len la dung duoc luon, khong phai setup lai tu dau.

Cac job khac (gian khoan dau, tho mo) van phai tu canh chinh. Job tho moc thi xem
muc rieng ben duoi.

Job Tho moc
-----------
Prompt "[E] KHAI THAC" gan vao GOC CAY chu khong phai HUD co dinh - do tren anh
chup that cua cung mot cai cay, prompt nam o y=841 roi y=944 khi camera doi goc.
Nen bot khong khoanh o co dinh ma quet trong mot BANG rong.

Vao tab "Moc" -> "Khoanh vung HUD...":
  1. Chup "Prompt khai thac": dung sat goc cay toi khi hien "[E] KHAI THAC".
  2. Khoanh no: trum CA o vuong chu E LAN chu "KHAI THAC". Lay du nen cung duoc.
  3. (Tuy chon) Khoanh "Vung quet" - mot bang RONG trum moi cho prompt co the hien.
     Bo qua thi bot tu lay 60%x50% giua man.

CHI CAN MOT MAU. Luc dang chat, dong chu doi thanh "DANG KHAI THAC"; mau duoc neo
vao MEP TRAI cua nhom chu nen no dem so voi "DANG KHAI" chu khong truot sang phan
"KHAI THAC" ben trong - het khop, va dung do la cach bot biet no dang ban.

App tu tach o phim ra khoi chu. O phim khong vao mau: ben trong no la chu E hay con
so dem nguoc, con quanh no la vong tien trinh 0->100 dang chay. Ca hai deu dang dong.

Nut "Thu do tren anh da chup" cho xem diem NCC ngay tren anh tinh - chinh nguong ma
khong phai vao game. Tab "Moc" cung hien diem doc truc tiep luc dang dung.

Chua khoanh thi job van chay, nhung chi go E mu theo nhip nhu job Tho mo.

Cay het go thi bot DUNG va bao - no khong tu di tim cay khac. Vi chi giu mot mau nen
bot khong phan biet duoc DANG CHAT voi HET CAY ngay lap tuc (ca hai deu la khong thay
prompt), no phai cho het gio moi dam bao.

Du lieu nguoi dung
------------------
Config, vung da khoanh va anh mau duoc luu o:
    %APPDATA%\GtaMiniGameBot
Lan chay dau tien app se chep config.json, fishing.json, wood.json, car-template.png
va cac thu muc fishing\, wood\ trong thu muc exe sang do lam mac dinh.

QUAN TRONG: app chi chep sang khi ben do CHUA CO file do. Ai da tung chay ban cu
roi thi se khong nhan duoc vung khoanh san - muon lay ban mac dinh moi thi xoa
thu muc %APPDATA%\GtaMiniGameBot roi chay lai.

Do cung la cach reset ve trang khi canh chinh loan xa nga.

Luu y
-----
- Ban zip khong kem anh chup tinh man hinh game (thu muc shots\). Bot chay binh
  thuong, chi khi vao form setup cop xe hoac form hoc chu so thi app se doi chup
  lai anh - bam nut chup trong form la xong.
- Go bo: xoa thu muc giai nen + thu muc %APPDATA%\GtaMiniGameBot. App khong ghi gi
  vao Registry.
